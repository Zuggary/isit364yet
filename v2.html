    What this is

    A joke with a scorekeeper. When Microsoft 365 has been down for one full day, 365 becomes 364. This site tracks every Microsoft 365 outage that Microsoft's public status page shows, starting 12 November 2026, and keeps the archive.

    When the clock starts

    8:17 AM Pacific on 12 November 2026 for the current outage. That is the start time Microsoft gave for incident EX1550543, as reported, so it is approximate.

    What counts as back up

    Microsoft says so. The clock stops when Microsoft's public status page shows no problem for Microsoft 365 on two checks in a row. "Works for me" does not count.

    Who keeps score

    A script reads that status page on a timer. It has not reported in yet. The stop time can be up to 20 minutes late, and your own mailbox may come back sooner or later than this page says. The sky on this page is worked out from the clock for the Pacific coast; it is not a weather report.
