Privacy

Is it 364 yet? is a parody page about email being down. It has no link to Microsoft. Here is what it keeps about you, in plain words.

What stays in your browser

Your game scores, your team pick (Team 5 or Team 4), your bingo card and the last game you played are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device. Clear your browser's site data and they are gone.

No accounts

There is no sign-in and no account. The page does not ask for your name or email.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees ordinary request logs, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser loads the page.

Ads, later

There are no ads yet. The site plans to show Google ads. When it does, Google and its partners will use cookies to show and measure ads. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. This page will be updated before any ad appears.
