Privacy

Is it 364 yet? is a parody page about email being down. It has no link to Microsoft. Here is what it keeps about you, in plain words.

What stays in your browser

Your game scores, your team pick (Team 5 or Team 4), your bingo card and the last game you played are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device; the counter below learns only that a side was picked or a game was started, not which. Clear your browser's site data and they are gone.

No accounts

There is no sign-in and no account. The page does not ask for your name or email.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees ordinary request logs, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser loads the page.

Counting visits

The home page loads one small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. Your scores and what you do inside a game are not sent.

The page also counts, as totals, how many visits reach each of these steps: clicked the credit link, picked a side (not which side), boosted the 5, opened a postcard, started a game, and whether the visit came from one of our own ads, by the ad's fixed name. Each step is sent as a fixed word from a short list in the page's code, such as "postcard". Cloudflare receives each step as its own request from your browser, the same way it receives the page view. We see only totals for each step: we cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next. It sets no cookie.

Ads

There are no ads on this site and no ad script loads. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.
